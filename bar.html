Bar

Bar

Amongst our three mini bars, our OUTDOOR bar (the purple inn), offers a wide range of vintage whiskies, and wines from all over the world. With a wide variety of Bhutanese local alcohol, and beer, we have a deep rooted passion for mixology, which allows us to offer cocktails and mocktail to suit each personality.

About Hotel

Lhayhuel sits perched majestically along the heart of the Paro Chhu, within the luscious village of Paro. Conveniently located ten minutes from the Airport and yet the view of the valley under your nose.
