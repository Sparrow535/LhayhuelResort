About Us

Lhayhuel Resort
Enjoy a Luxury Experience

Lhayhuel began as a passion project focused on quality and experience over quantity and revenue. Our vision is to offer exceptional care and service, drawing on the rich traditions of our valley and honoring the legacy of our ancestors.

Located in the heart of Paro, just ten minutes from the airport, Lhayhuel invites you to experience the valley’s beauty and bounty. Enjoy a culinary journey with local ingredients, relax in our luxury hotel spa with bespoke pampering, and savor vintage wines and whiskeys at our outdoor bar. Join us in celebrating the essence of Paro, and make this experience as much yours as it is ours.
